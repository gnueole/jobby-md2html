Why Markdown (MD)?

Markdown separates content from formatting. This guarantees that **Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)** can parse your resume's text flawlessly without getting tripped up by complex hidden document structures.

AI-Powered CV Writing

Markdown is the ultimate asset when collaborating with AI tools (like Gemini, ChatGPT, or Claude). We highly recommend using AI to write and refine your CV. Just explicitly prompt it to "generate in MD format" for direct copy-paste.

Privacy & Data Policy (GDPR / RGPD)

Jobby is a personal, non-commercial project. We care deeply about your privacy and adhere to GDPR / RGPD guidelines:

  • Private CV Content: We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your resume text, candidate details, or compiled files. Your data is processed entirely locally within your browser.
  • Local Drafts: Your markdown drafts and design choices are saved locally in your browser's localStorage.
  • Anonymous Telemetry: Telemetry collection is strictly anonymous and does not capture any personal identifiers.
  • User Feedback (Voluntary): We only receive details you explicitly submit through the feedback form.

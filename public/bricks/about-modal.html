✍️ Why Markdown (MD)?

Markdown separates content from formatting. This guarantees that **Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)** can parse your resume's text flawlessly without getting tripped up by complex hidden document structures (like tables or text boxes in Word/PDFs). It also keeps your resume future-proof, portable, and easy to edit.

🤖 AI-Powered CV Writing

Markdown is the ultimate asset when collaborating with AI tools (like Gemini, ChatGPT, or Claude). We highly recommend manipulating and tailoring your CV using your favorite AI. Just remember to explicitly prompt it to "generate in MD format", so you can copy and paste the raw text cleanly directly into Jobby without formatting loss!

🔒 Privacy & Data Policy (GDPR / RGPD)

Jobby is a personal, non-commercial project. We care deeply about your privacy and adhere to GDPR / RGPD guidelines:

  • Private CV Content: We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your resume text, candidate details, or compiled files. Your data is processed entirely locally within your browser.
  • Local Drafts: Your markdown drafts and design choices are saved locally in your browser's localStorage (or directly in your own files if using the File System Access API).
  • Anonymous Telemetry: • Purpose: To analyze design preset adoption, monitor ATS scorecard failure rates (to refine compliance rules), track feature clicks, and monitor compiler rendering performance.
    • Privacy Level: Highly restricted and anonymous. A persistent, random anonymous ID is stored in your browser's localStorage. No personal data, names, IP addresses, or location trackers are collected or linked to telemetry.
  • User Feedback (Voluntary): • Purpose: To process user suggestions, bug reports, and quality ratings.
    • Privacy Level: We only receive details you explicitly submit through the form (rating, category, text, and optional name/email).
